# Contributing and collaboration

This repository supports discussion of the PVMouse 3.0 white paper and improvements to its companion documentation. No runnable implementation is included.

## Scientific feedback

Open an issue with a descriptive title and include:

- The relevant PDF page, section, figure or companion file.
- The claim, assumption or proposed method you are discussing.
- The evidence, alternative interpretation or suggested correction.
- A testable comparison or experiment where applicable.

Distinguish established observations, modeling assumptions, proposed capabilities and independently validated results. For evaluation proposals, state available inputs, target readouts, independent units, baseline comparisons and possible leakage.

## Documentation changes

Small corrections, clearer navigation and translation improvements are welcome as pull requests. Keep English and Chinese summaries aligned and preserve the paper's qualifications about scope and validation. Identify scientific wording changes explicitly so the authors can review them. Suggested changes to the white paper itself should be discussed with the authors rather than silently replacing the published PDF.

## Data, model and experimental collaboration

For joint work, describe the biological system, perturbations, organs, age coverage, modalities, pairing structure and proposed validation task. Contact the authors before exchanging data requiring permissions or agreements. Use public issues for information intended to be public.
